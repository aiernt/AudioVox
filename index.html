The Band

Flannel. Feedback.
Front Row Chaos.

AudioVox is Charlotte’s ultimate 90’s rock cover band, throwing it back to the era of flannel, distortion pedals, and songs everyone still screams every word to.

From grunge anthems and alt-rock staples to pop-punk singalongs and a few 2000’s favorites for good measure, we bring the raw energy of the decade that changed music forever — straight to your bar, backyard, or big stage.

No backing tracks. No auto-tune. Just real amps, real drums, and a setlist built to make a room lose its mind.
