Flyers

The flyers sit in two piles on the home page, left and right. The top flyer in each pile is in front; the ones under it peek out behind at an angle. Clicking any flyer on the site opens them all full size. Give a flyer its show date and its stamp (like ROCKED!) appears by itself the day after the show; give it a take-down date and it comes off the site that day. Changes show on the site straight away.

About the band

The band photo next to “Flannel. Feedback. Front Row Chaos.” and the “Meet The Band” lineup. On the site, clicking a member's photo opens their bio.

Band photo

Gallery

The photos in the “Live Shots” carousel. Drag photos to change the order (or use the arrows). Big photos are shrunk automatically before uploading. A yellow border means the photo has no description yet. Untick “Show in the carousel” to keep a photo here without showing it on the site.

      Song requests

      Songs fans have asked for on the Songs We Should Learn page, most votes first. “We learned it” adds the song to Songs we play (so it can't be requested again) and takes it off this list.

      Songs we play

      Fans can't request these; the song page says “We play this one!” instead. A song is matched by its title, so cover versions by other bands are caught too. For common titles (like “Black” or “Higher”), tick “Artist must match” so only that band's song is blocked.
